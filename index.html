What does Sreedher V do?

I’m an SEO Analyst and Digital Marketing Specialist based in Coimbatore, India. I work across SEO, AEO, GEO, Google and Meta Ads, GA4 analytics, AI-assisted website development and Canva creative design.

What are AEO and GEO?

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) structures content so search engines and voice assistants can lift it as a direct answer, such as a featured snippet. Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) makes a brand easy for AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews to understand, trust and cite.

Can you build and launch a website?

Yes. I build websites with AI-assisted development, make them fast, responsive and SEO-ready, then deploy them live with version control, a custom domain and Google Search Console set up.
